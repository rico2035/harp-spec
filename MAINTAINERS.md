# HARP Maintainers

This file lists the people who triage issues, review RFCs and pull requests, cut releases, and run the conformance suite. The structure is intentionally small at v0.1 and broadens as the Working Group forms. See `GOVERNANCE.md` for roles and the decision process.

Editor seats hold the pen on spec text and schemas. As providers, payers, clearinghouses, EHRs, and agent builders adopt HARP and co-sign a major version, maintainer and editor seats open to people outside Neomics, per `GOVERNANCE.md` section 2.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who land meaningful RFCs and reviews over time, and who represent an organization implementing HARP, can be nominated by an existing maintainer. The maintainers decide by consensus. The aim is a Working Group with a majority of seats held by people outside Neomics, which is also one of the thresholds that triggers the donation process in `GOVERNANCE.md` section 10.
